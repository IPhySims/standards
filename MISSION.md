# Mission & Vision — International Physics Simulation Society

---

## Our Vision

> **To become the world's most trusted and authoritative repository of validated physics simulations, advancing science and education through open, reproducible, and peer-reviewed computational physics.**

We envision a world where every physicist, educator, student, and researcher has access to a curated collection of high-quality, verified physics simulations — freely available, rigorously reviewed, and universally accessible.

---

## Our Mission

The **International Physics Simulation Society (IPhySims)** exists to:

### 1. Curate Excellence
Establish and maintain the gold standard for physics simulation quality through a rigorous, transparent peer-review process. Every simulation bearing the IPhySims badge has been verified for scientific accuracy, reproducibility, and educational value.

### 2. Champion Open Science
Promote open-source development and free access to physics simulation tools. We believe that the tools for understanding the physical universe should be accessible to all, without barriers.

### 3. Build Community
Foster a global, inclusive community of simulation developers, physicists, educators, and learners who collaborate to advance computational physics.

### 4. Advance Education
Support physics education at every level — from secondary school to graduate research — by providing simulation resources that make abstract concepts tangible and explorable.

### 5. Ensure Reproducibility
Verify that every curated simulation produces consistent, reproducible results across platforms, contributing to the broader scientific reproducibility movement.

## Why IPhySims Matters

### The Problem
- Physics simulations are scattered across repositories, personal websites, and proprietary platforms
- Quality varies enormously — many simulations contain errors or misleading results
- No standardized review process exists for educational simulations
- Students and educators struggle to find trustworthy simulation resources
- Reproducibility crises affect computational science broadly

### The IPhySims Solution
- **Centralized Repository**: A single, authoritative source for validated simulations
- **Peer Review**: Every simulation is reviewed by domain experts before publication
- **Quality Badges**: Clear indicators of verified quality and accuracy
- **Community Standards**: Established best practices for simulation development
- **Open Access**: All curated simulations are freely available to everyone

## Strategic Goals (2025–2030)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Year 1)
- [ ] Establish organizational structure and governance
- [ ] Register domain and launch website
- [ ] Recruit founding members and editorial board
- [ ] Define review standards and submission process
- [ ] Launch initial repository with 20+ curated simulations
- [ ] Establish social media and communication channels

### Phase 2: Growth (Years 2–3)
- [ ] Reach 100+ curated simulations across all major physics domains
- [ ] Recruit 500+ active members worldwide
- [ ] Establish partnerships with 20+ universities and institutions
- [ ] Host first annual IPhySims conference
- [ ] Launch educational certification program
- [ ] Develop API for integration with learning management systems

### Phase 3: Authority (Years 4–5)
- [ ] Become the recognized standard for physics simulation quality
- [ ] Reach 1,000+ curated simulations
- [ ] Establish regional chapters in 5+ countries
- [ ] Publish first IPhySims Journal of Computational Physics Education
- [ ] Advise international bodies on simulation standards
- [ ] Achieve formal recognition from physics societies (APS, IUPAP, etc.)
