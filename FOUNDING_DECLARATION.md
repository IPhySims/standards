# Founding Declaration — International Physics Simulation Society (IPhySims)

## Official Founding Date
**September 29, 2026**

## Declaration

On this date, the **International Physics Simulation Society (IPhySims)** was established as an open-source, non-profit international organization dedicated to serving as the global authority for the curation, validation, and certification of physics simulations.

## Founding Documents

The following documents were created and published on the founding date:

1. **Charter** — The organizational constitution establishing governance, membership, and operations
2. **Mission & Vision** — The strategic direction and goals for 2025–2030
3. **Review Standards** — The peer review criteria and process for simulation certification
4. **Contribution Guidelines** — How to submit simulations and participate
5. **Code of Conduct** — Community standards and expectations
6. **Website** — Public-facing information at iphysim.org

## Founding Principles

IPhySims was founded on these principles:

1. **Openness** — All curated simulations must be open-source
2. **Rigor** — Every simulation undergoes peer review by domain experts
3. **Accessibility** — Free access for all, regardless of institution or geography
4. **Reproducibility** — Verified results across platforms
5. **Community** — Governed by and for the physics community

## First Actions

- Domain registered: **iphysim.org**
- GitHub organization created: **github.com/IPhySims**
- Website deployed
- Founding documents published under CC BY-SA 4.0 license

## Call to Action

We invite physicists, educators, developers, and students worldwide to:
- Join as founding members
- Submit simulations for certification
- Volunteer as peer reviewers
- Partner with IPhySims
