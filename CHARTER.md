# Charter of the International Physics Simulation Society (IPhySims)

## Preamble

We, the undersigned, establish the **International Physics Simulation Society** (hereinafter referred to as "IPhySims" or "the Society") as an open-source, non-profit international organization dedicated to the curation, validation, and dissemination of high-quality physics simulations for the global scientific and educational community.

Recognizing the critical role that computational physics simulations play in modern science, education, and engineering, we establish this Society to serve as an authoritative international body that ensures the quality, reproducibility, and accessibility of physics simulation resources.

---

## Article I — Name and Identity

### Section 1.1 — Official Name
The official name of the organization shall be:  
**International Physics Simulation Society (IPhySims)**

### Section 1.2 — Abbreviation
The abbreviation **IPhySims** shall be used in all official communications, publications, and digital platforms.

### Section 1.3 — Legal Nature
IPhySims is established as a non-profit, open-source organization operating for educational and scientific purposes. It shall not distribute profits to members, directors, or officers.

---

## Article II — Mission and Purpose

### Section 2.1 — Mission Statement
The mission of IPhySims is to:
1. Curate and maintain a authoritative repository of validated physics simulations
2. Establish rigorous peer-review standards for simulation quality and accuracy
3. Promote open-source development and free access to physics simulation tools
4. Foster international collaboration among physicists, educators, and developers
5. Advance physics education and research through computational methods

### Section 2.2 — Core Objectives
The Society shall pursue the following objectives:
- **Quality Assurance**: Establish and maintain review standards for all submitted simulations
- **Open Access**: Ensure all curated simulations remain freely accessible
- **Reproducibility**: Verify that simulations produce consistent, reproducible results
- **Education**: Support physics education at all levels through simulation resources
- **Community**: Build a global community of simulation developers and users
- **Standards**: Develop and promote best practices in physics simulation development

---

## Article III — Scope of Activities

### Section 3.1 — Simulation Categories
The Society shall curate simulations across all branches of physics, including but not limited to:
- Classical Mechanics
- Electromagnetism
- Quantum Mechanics
- Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics
- Optics and Photonics
- Nuclear and Particle Physics
- Astrophysics and Cosmology
- Condensed Matter Physics
- Fluid Dynamics
- Computational Physics Methods

### Section 3.2 — Activities
The Society shall engage in:
1. **Peer Review**: Evaluation of submitted simulations for scientific accuracy, code quality, and educational value
2. **Publication**: Maintenance of a curated repository of approved simulations
3. **Documentation**: Provision of comprehensive documentation for all curated simulations
4. **Education**: Development of educational resources and tutorials
5. **Conferences**: Organization of annual conferences and workshops
6. **Publications**: Publication of journals, newsletters, and technical reports
7. **Collaboration**: Partnership with universities, research institutions, and organizations

---

## Article IV — Membership

### Section 4.1 — Membership Categories
The Society shall have the following membership categories:

| Category | Description | Voting Rights |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Founding Members** | Original signatories of this Charter | Full voting rights |
| **Full Members** | Active contributors and reviewers | Full voting rights |
| **Associate Members** | Users and occasional contributors | No voting rights |
| **Honorary Members** | Distinguished individuals recognized by the Society | Advisory role |
| **Student Members** | Enrolled students in accredited institutions | Full voting rights |

### Section 4.2 — Membership Requirements
- All members must agree to the Society's Code of Conduct
- Full Members must have contributed at least 3 reviewed simulations or 10 peer reviews
- Membership is open to individuals worldwide regardless of nationality, affiliation, or background
- Membership shall be free of charge for Student Members; other categories may have nominal fees as determined by the Governing Board

### Section 4.3 — Membership Rights and Responsibilities
All members shall:
- Have access to Society resources and platforms
- Participate in community discussions and events
- Contribute to the Society's mission
- Uphold the Society's standards and values
- Follow the Code of Conduct

---

## Article V — Governance Structure

### Section 5.1 — Governing Board
The Society shall be governed by a **Governing Board** consisting of:
- **President**: Chief executive officer, responsible for overall leadership
- **Vice President**: Assists the President and assumes duties in their absence
- **Secretary**: Maintains records, minutes, and official communications
- **Treasurer**: Manages finances, budgets, and financial reporting
- **Technical Director**: Oversees technical infrastructure and review processes
- **Education Director**: Oversees educational initiatives and outreach
- **Members-at-Large**: 3 additional board members elected by the membership

### Section 5.2 — Election of Officers
- The President, Vice President, and Technical Director shall be elected by full members
- Other officers shall be appointed by the President with Board approval
- Terms shall be 2 years, with a maximum of 2 consecutive terms for elected positions
- Elections shall be conducted annually for staggered terms

### Section 5.3 — Editorial Board
An **Editorial Board** shall be established to oversee the peer review process:
- Composed of recognized experts in various physics domains
- Responsible for maintaining review standards
- Appointed by the Technical Director with Board approval
- Serves 3-year terms

### Section 5.4 — Decision Making
- Routine decisions: Simple majority of participating Board members
- Major decisions (charter amendments, mergers, dissolution): 2/3 supermajority of all full members
- Emergency decisions: President may act with ratification by the Board within 30 days

---

## Article VI — Peer Review Process

### Section 6.1 — Review Standards
All simulations submitted for inclusion in the IPhySims repository shall be evaluated on:

1. **Scientific Accuracy** (40%)
   - Correctness of physical models and equations
   - Appropriate approximations and assumptions
   - Validation against known analytical solutions or experimental data

2. **Code Quality** (25%)
   - Clean, readable, and well-documented code
   - Proper version control and dependency management
   - Adherence to open-source licensing

3. **Reproducibility** (20%)
   - Clear instructions for execution
   - Documented parameters and initial conditions
   - Consistent results across different platforms

4. **Educational Value** (15%)
   - Clear documentation and tutorials
   - Appropriate for stated educational level
   - Demonstrates physical concepts effectively

### Section 6.2 — Review Process
1. **Submission**: Author submits simulation via the official submission portal
2. **Initial Screening**: Technical staff verifies completeness and basic functionality
3. **Peer Review**: At least 2 independent reviewers evaluate the simulation
4. **Revision**: Author addresses reviewer feedback
5. **Final Decision**: Editorial Board makes acceptance/rejection decision
6. **Publication**: Accepted simulations are published in the IPhySims repository

### Section 6.3 — Reviewer Qualifications
Reviewers must:
- Hold at least a Master's degree in physics or related field
- Have published at least 2 peer-reviewed simulations with IPhySims
- Complete reviewer training provided by the Society
- Maintain active reviewer status through regular review activity

## Article VII — Intellectual Property and Licensing

### Section 7.1 — Open Source Requirement
All simulations accepted into the IPhySims repository must be released under an approved open-source license:
- **Preferred**: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
- **Accepted**: MIT License, Apache License 2.0, BSD Licenses
- **Not Accepted**: Proprietary or restrictive licenses

### Section 7.2 — Author Rights
- Authors retain copyright of their original work
- Authors grant IPhySims a perpetual, non-exclusive license to distribute the work
- Authors may continue to develop and distribute their simulations independently

### Section 7.3 — IPhySims Brand
- The IPhySims name, logo, and trademarks are property of the Society
- Use of IPhySims branding requires written permission from the Governing Board
- Curated simulations may display the "IPhySims Verified" badge

---

## Article VIII — Finances

### Section 8.1 — Funding Sources
The Society may receive funding from:
- Membership dues (if applicable)
- Grants from foundations, governments, and international organizations
- Donations from individuals and corporations
- Conference registration fees
- Merchandise sales
- Sponsorships (with Board approval and ethical guidelines)

### Section 8.2 — Financial Management
- The Treasurer shall maintain accurate financial records
- Annual budgets must be approved by the Governing Board
- Financial statements shall be audited annually
- All expenditures must align with the Society's mission

### Section 8.3 — Non-Profit Status
- No portion of net earnings shall benefit any private individual
- The Society shall not engage in substantial lobbying activities
- The Society shall comply with applicable non-profit laws in its jurisdiction(s) of operation

---

## Article IX — Amendments and Dissolution

### Section 9.1 — Charter Amendments
This Charter may be amended by:
1. Proposal submitted to the Governing Board
2. 30-day notice period for member review
3. Approval by 2/3 supermajority of voting members in a formal vote

### Section 9.2 — Dissolution
The Society may be dissolved by:
1. Proposal by the Governing Board or petition by 1/3 of full members
2. 60-day notice period
3. Approval by 3/4 supermajority of all full members
4. Upon dissolution, assets shall be transferred to a compatible non-profit organization

---

## Article X — Miscellaneous

### Section 10.1 — Official Languages
The official language of the Society shall be English. Translations may be provided for accessibility.

### Section 10.2 — Non-Discrimination Policy
The Society shall not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or political affiliation.

### Section 10.3 — Conflict of Interest
All Board members and reviewers must disclose potential conflicts of interest and recuse themselves from related decisions.

### Section 10.4 — Privacy Policy
The Society shall maintain a privacy policy protecting member data and complying with applicable data protection regulations (including GDPR).
